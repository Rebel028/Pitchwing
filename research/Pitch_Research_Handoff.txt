DESIGN DECISION
First R&D candidate: short fixed synthesis support plus long past-heavy
analysis, coherent spectral-region transport, confidence-weighted time and
frequency phase guidance, linked stereo and measured onset handling.
Initial48kHz/N2048/L384/H96 scheduler383samples. Investigate nonuniform complex
subbands separately; no Compose-clone claim. Unity scaffold proves scheduling
and reconstruction only. Pitch modification may cause aliasing or stale attacks.

CRITICAL FALSIFIER
Two unresolved tones: moving one carrier while preserving its envelope retains
original beat spacing; true transposition requires scaled spacing. Test low
close dyads/chord transitions before trusting isolated-tone accuracy.
