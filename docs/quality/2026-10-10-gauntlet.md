# Gauntlet on the quality redesign choices (2026-10-10)

Four read-only adversarial reviewers (formalist, historian, practitioner,
consumer advocate) attacked the design choices A–H listed in the brief below,
with `src/LiveEngine.cpp`, the two quality reports and `docs/live-engine.md` as
ground truth. Reports are condensed here; the synthesis is at the end.

## Brief (what was attacked)

- A. Region boundaries: soft $\cos^2$ masks at magnitude valleys vs brick-wall
  midpoints vs per-bin accumulated phase.
- B. Synthesis: B1 direct per-bin evaluation over the 384-sample support;
  B2 Laroche–Dolson single IFFT (hop-rate control); B3 keep per-region IFFTs.
- C. Gated ancestry with birth $\theta=0$, zombies, admission hysteresis, confidence.
- D. DC policy B (5 Hz HPF in the engine, POU DC term, bin 0 excluded) vs
  policy A (fixed untranslated sub-25 Hz region); 1-bin low-side taper.
- E. Past-only onset score (FFT 512 flux) with region-local θ re-anchor and
  band widening.
- F. Low-band zoom estimator (decimate ×8, 341 ms, 2.9 Hz bins) feeding
  parametric resynthesis of unresolved low regions.
- G. Window-matched / phase-increment frequency estimator replacing the
  log-parabola + $\pm 2$-bin IF gate.
- H. Sequencing A→C→G→D, then B1, E, F.

## Formalist (structure, invariants)

Verdict: the only real invariant is "a bin's output is continuous iff it keeps
the same owner region and that owner's θ sequence is unbroken" (`renderRegion`
385–434, θ advance 321). A, C, E, F each break it at a boundary the brief
does not define; D-policy-B breaks exact unity; H makes B1's null test and C's
gate untestable.

- A: a ramp bin shared by regions p,q has amplitude $|w + (1-w)e^{j(\theta_p-\theta_q)}|$
  — a steady notch at every boundary once θ diverge after any nonzero control,
  plus an in-frame beat at $|\delta_p-\delta_q|$. With peaks 2–3 bins apart (every low
  harmonic series; the proposed 300+345 Hz fixture) a 1-bin ramp covers the
  peak bins themselves: undefined when $k_q-k_p \leq 2w$. Death collapses the
  ramp ($w \to 1$ step), so A smooths boundary moves only, not births/deaths.
  Valley argmin undefined on monotone intervals. "B1 needed by A" is false:
  weighted bins can go into both per-region IFFTs today. Per-bin accumulated
  phase is the only option that restores the invariant for bins that change
  owner, and it keeps exact unity.
- B: B1's flat cost is per hop; the scheduler (224–227) is region-granular and
  the top region can span ~700 bins → one 20 µs slot at 1-sample blocks.
  $e^{j\omega_k n}$ over 384 samples is control-independent: a $1024 \times 384$ twiddle
  table (6 MB) makes B1 exact, no recurrence drift. B1's null test is only
  meaningful before A/C/G/D change the output (H contradiction). B2 violates
  the per-sample control contract and should not be listed. B3's cap
  hysteresis needs stable region identity (depends on C).
- C: birth $\theta=0$ is wrong for admission-threshold crossings — the bin was
  already emitted under its previous owner's θ (3 frames in flight), so $\theta=0$
  steps its phase: a click exactly when C fires. Total rule that needs no
  gate: ancestor := the previous region that owned bin $k_p$ (every bin had
  exactly one owner, 385–386). A 1-bin gate re-births on every IF toggle
  (303–306) if C precedes G. Hysteresis relative to `largest` (264) moves
  with the loudest note's decay; needs an absolute/slewed reference. Zombies:
  must θ keep advancing? which wins, a zombie at 1.0 bin or a live region at
  1.1? Unity: 310 zeroes θ in unity frames and 374–376 stores them as
  ancestors; caveat 5 (unity dip) is absent from A–H.
- D: an HPF inside the engine breaks the exact unity path (delayed output ≠
  input); feeding only the analysis needs a second buffer (352/363 share
  `time[]`) and leaves a DC step at the unity↔nonunity transition.
  "$\mathrm{dc} \cdot \mathrm{Hann}/4$" sums to 0.5 with hop $L/4$; the code's $A \cdot S=(1-\cos)/4$ (119–121)
  sums to 1 — use the code formula. $\mathrm{dc}=X[0]/N=\mathrm{mean}(x \cdot A)=0.636c$: POU alone
  leaves the gain wrong, needs $/\mathrm{mean}(A)$. Policy A's 2-bin region is the
  worst case of caveat 3's $1/A$ tail amplification unless synthesised with
  $(1-\cos)/4$ instead of $S$. A 1-bin low-side taper leaves bins toggling,
  softer; conjugate mirroring (Laroche–Dolson §3.4) is total.
- E: FFT(512) sees an attack immediately but the analysis window weights those
  samples by $A \to 0.008$, so the spectrum contains it 2–4 frames later;
  re-anchoring on the detection frame rotates the sustain (AM) and the region
  is no longer "young" when the attack arrives. Band widening at the one band
  holding all guitar fundamentals = Kraft's global reset at band scale.
  Caveat 8 attributes the lag to the window, which θ cannot restore. Measure
  first: lag on the region path at $u \approx 10^{-9}$ vs exact unity.
- F: $a_i(n)=a_i \cdot |z_p[n]|/\mathrm{rms}$ AM-modulates the oscillators at the original
  beat rate (the defect itself). Parametric output is not $x \cdot A$, so OLA with
  $S=\mathrm{Hann}/(4A)$ multiplies it by $1/A$ (×122 at the tail): must use
  $(1-\cos)/4$. Parametric↔rigid switching overlaps two phase conventions in
  the ring with no matching rule. Stereo oscillator phases undefined; with A,
  a zoom peak on a ramp is double-counted.
- G: claimed the IF estimate is unbiased for stationary tones and the bias is
  a Python-era measurement. **Refuted by the probe in this session**: the IF
  estimate is corrupted by the negative-frequency image (152–681 cents pp at
  bins 1.3–3.5, mean −16 c at E2); see
  `docs/results/2026-10-10-estimator-probe.txt`. Valid points: the −7 st
  measurement is also confounded by the dropped bin 1 (392); "3 lobe bins" is
  undefined at bin 1 and for pairs 2–3 bins apart; stereo (per channel or
  joint cross product) unstated.
- H: A before B1 invalidates B1's null test; C before G feeds C from the
  toggling estimator; D changes every fixture baseline so "no regression"
  needs per-metric tolerances; the unity-transition dip is missing; A's
  fixture is degenerate for A; bit-exactness with the static Engine was never
  asserted (`docs/live-engine.md`).
- Held: trapezoid θ (321) and in-frame suffix phase (403) are consistent under
  mid-hop control changes, including for two-owner bins and frame-end
  re-anchors; B1 is mathematically identical to the per-region IFFT when done
  per region.

## Consumer advocate (ShiftKey, the player)

Verdict: B2, D-policy-B, F and the C/E trigger gates silently rewrite promises
in `docs/live-engine.md` that ShiftKey builds on; H tests the fragile ones in
the wrong order. Most likely burn: a held low dyad (primary requirement) makes
C/E re-trigger at every beat null, turning a steady defect into a ~20 Hz phasey
flutter.

- A: trades intermittent churn for a steady artefact — each ramp bin is
  emitted twice at two deltas; for E2 harmonics a 1-bin ramp covers ~60 % of
  the inter-peak space, second copy displaced by $(\omega_p-\omega_q)(r-1)$ (~41 Hz at −12)
  at skirt level, on every low chord all the time. Valley wanders by bins per
  frame on beating pairs. A does not need B1 (`research-options` §3 says IFFT
  count unchanged). `live-engine.md` "rigid region translation" would no
  longer be literally true.
- B1: "flat cost" and "per-bin δ with per-sample control" cannot both hold —
  per-bin $\delta_k$ with per-sample $u[n]$ means ~$1024 \times 384$ sincos per hop on a
  ShiftKey ±24 curve updated every sample. The factorised form
  $y = \mathrm{Re} \sum_p M_p[n] \sum_{k \in p} X[k]e^{j\omega_k n}$ is flat but δ is per region again.
  Measure with a per-sample random control fixture. B2 breaks per-sample
  control, "no hidden ramp", and the curve-clock measurement (row 14); fine
  control (±1 st) makes every shift fractional so the −51 dBc sidebands are
  permanent; a ±24 curve over 20 ms becomes 10 steps of 2.4 st. Reject
  without a contract change. B3: CPU bound becomes data-dependent silently.
- C: beat nulls are the hole — E2/G#2 beat at 21 Hz; around each null the
  merged peak passes near zero within ~2 ms, the 12 dB gate fails, the region
  is re-born with $\theta=0$: the caveat-5 6 ms dip 21× per second on exactly the
  primary material. Add E2+G#2 and E2+A2 with birth counters. Relative
  hysteresis against a moving `largest` is not hysteresis. Dive-bomb/slide
  (~1.6 bin/hop) re-births every frame — velocity-aware gate needed. Do
  zombies occupy `regions[64]` slots? `reset()` must clear ages/zombies.
- D: policy B silently breaks exact delayed reconstruction at unity;
  ShiftKey's dry-path null test at pitch 0 would show −24 dB residual at
  82 Hz, −18 dB at 41 Hz, and the developer would blame `latencySamples()`.
  DC steps (plug-in, mute pop) become 32 ms tails. Policy A keeps unity exact.
  If B: change the unity test and `live-engine.md` in the same commit and
  HPF the fixture reference identically. Low-side taper and the POU DC term:
  no consumer effect.
- E: "rose >6 dB in one hop" fires on every emergence from a beat null;
  action ($\theta=0$ + widening) detunes the low chord for 2–3 frames ~20× per
  second. 16-frame median is ~0 after `reset()`/mute, so every pluck from
  silence gets 32 ms of re-anchoring on its sustain; `eps` is load-bearing
  and undefined. No vibrato case in the proposed test (6 Hz vibrato period is
  5× the median window). A false trigger sounds exactly like caveat 5.
- F: the region envelope used for $a_i(n)$ is the original beat, so the
  oscillators get AM at the old spacing (~−13 dB sidebands for E2+A2). For
  0.2–0.3 s after every strum the zoom window still holds the previous
  chord's peaks → old chord's low notes under the new one; at 120 bpm eighths
  the model is wrong permanently; $c_p = \min(1, \mathrm{age}/4)$ is 8 ms and cannot
  gate a 170 ms lag. Parametric↔rigid handoff is a phase step (6 ms dip)
  ~100–170 ms into every held low note. Stereo: one $\phi_i$ per component
  collapses the low band to mono; anti-phase $L=-R$ at 82 Hz comes out in
  phase. `reset()` must clear the decimated history.
- G: holds for the consumer (fixes a bias the ±1 fine control cannot work
  around). Open: does C's gate compare against slewed or raw ω?
- H: order G→C; D-policy-B cannot "keep tests green"; caveat 5 (unity dip)
  is the most probable live complaint and the failure mode C/E/F all
  reproduce on mis-trigger — build that fixture first. A median-type baseline
  gate can call "intermittent crackle → steady sidebands" an improvement;
  keep the listening excerpt as a hard gate.

## Practitioner (year-one maintainer)

Verdict: the individual fixes are mostly sound; as sequenced the plan builds
six phase-discontinuity sources with ~25 inline magic numbers, no per-region
trace, and a test oracle (captured-waveform parity, `live-engine.md`
150–152) that dies at step A. Fix the harness before touching A.

- A: weights summing to 1 do not give unit gain (steady amplitude beat on
  boundary bins at the differential rate — mechanism 3's tremolo
  redistributed). Undefined for peaks ≤2 bins apart; needs
  $\mathrm{ramp} = \min(w, \text{half-gap}-1)$ and a rule for gap 1. Keep midpoints and add
  the ramp (the ramp, not the valley, is the churn fix). `taper` (101, 396)
  is one shared array; overlapping masks need two weights per boundary bin.
- B1 is not a CPU win for guitar: break-even $R \approx 28$ at 48 kHz, $R \approx 50$ at 96 kHz;
  typical sparse spectra run 10–25 regions, so B1 raises median cost and
  only lowers the 64-cap worst case. Overrun fix for the measured 96 kHz case
  is B3 (cap 32 + membership hysteresis). "Skip bins below floor" defeats
  SIMD. Double needs no re-anchor (drift ~1e-13 over 384 steps); float does;
  AVX2 4×f64 vs NEON 2×f64 — decide precision first. Portability trap:
  `std::complex<double>` multiply goes through `__muldc3` on libstdc++/libc++
  without `-fcx-limited-range`, MSVC inlines, Apple Clang contracts FMA by
  default — write the recurrence in real arithmetic. x86 denormals in a float
  recurrence (no FTZ/DAZ in an engine-only scope). The analysis burst
  (2 FFTs, hypot ×1025, sort ≤500, `findRegions`) sits in the hop-boundary
  sample and B1 does not touch it; E and F add to it. Add a per-sample cycle
  histogram to the harness and report the max.
- C: C before G makes mechanism 9 louder (IF 2-bin jump → re-birth kick).
  Relative hysteresis on a moving `largest` → regions die because something
  else got loud. Zombie θ must keep integrating with last ω and `hopArea` or
  the re-link is worse than a birth. `confidence` has three consumers (E, F,
  G) and an unspecified formula — define once with a fixture or give each its
  own gate. Five new inline constants; no tuning struct exists.
- D: HPF-into-history changes the unity contract and its test; HPF-into-
  analysis-only gives a DC step on every return to pitch 0. Bin 0 exclusion
  already exists (385 `begin = 1`). Policy B leaves the real low-end defect
  (DC-lobe bins 1–2 translated, E1 ghost) untouched and reopens when bass
  lands; policy A has a bass story — measure its B0/E1 lobe-capture risk
  instead of assuming it. POU DC term and low-side taper hold (taper inherits
  caveat 10's frame-end shortcut).
- E: FFT(512) bins are 94 Hz; five low fundamentals share two detector bins,
  so one new low note marks the whole band and re-anchors every sustained low
  region — the band-scoped global reset the not-to-do list rejects. `eps` in
  absolute units with no level normalisation: $T \approx 3$ is calibrated to one DI.
  Widen/unwiden is deliberate mechanism-2 churn after every pick. "Do
  nothing" is defensible: ship A/D/G, re-measure the lag, decide E on real DI.
- F: second rate-dependent geometry (48 kHz-specific); model switch is a
  waveform discontinuity with no crossfade; on strummed progressions the low
  band alternates models ~40 % of the time and the 3 s sustained-dyad test
  cannot see it. Matrix pencil needs an eigen/SVD routine (dependency → ask)
  and is an 8e6-flop burst every 16 ms unless sliced. A third phase
  integrator next to $\theta_p$ and B1's $\theta_k$.
- G: holds for isolated tones; single-sinusoid fit is wrong on dyads in a new
  way and the slew only slows the warble — write that into the acceptance.
  G's low test tones (bins 2–3) sit on the DC lobe D changes: run D first.
  The asymmetric window's lobe is not even in ω: table must cover both
  signs.
- H: step 0 missing — none of the ~20 fixtures has been run on the current
  engine; record current numbers, gate on relative change per commit, promote
  to absolute thresholds only after listening ties a number to an audible
  effect. The captured-waveform oracle dies at A; B1 is the one step that can
  be null-tested, so do it while the oracle is valid. Suggested order:
  step 0 (harness, internal tuning struct with per-mechanism enables,
  preallocated per-frame per-region trace ring drained off-thread) → D → B1
  → G → C → A → E → F; C/E/F each need a combined fixture with its
  predecessor. Missing from A–H: caveat 5 (unity dip) and caveat 10 / options
  §4 (alias taper from the support maximum), both cheaper than anything
  listed. Portability claims stay unverified until a native Windows/macOS run
  is recorded.

## Historian (prior art)

Verdict: the plan rebuilds the 1986–2003 sinusoidal-modelling heuristic stack
(MQ/PARSHL tracking, oscillator banks, flux onsets, parametric low band) on an
identity-locked phase vocoder, and defers the one defect that is a 100-cent
pitch error (unresolved low dyads) to last while spending first on items that
trade clicks for AM.

- A: Laroche–Dolson §3.2 found midpoint vs lowest-magnitude boundaries
  immaterial; the ecosystem answer to peaks switching regions (Rubber Band
  `m_prevPeaks`, PVDR heap) was per-bin phase state, not softer partitions.
  Ramp bins emit the skirt at two frequencies — the Ocean split-lobe artefact
  (D04). Enforce the options doc's own gate (measure first; drop if passing).
- B: motivation is a WSL 96 kHz overrun (not target-native). Profile two cheap
  suspects first: line 418 zeroes 2048 complex per region per channel (4 MB of
  writes per hop at 64 regions; clear only the previous band) and the sort at
  274. B1 is a 1024-oscillator bank; Rodet–Depalle 1992 $\mathrm{FFT}^{-1}$ synthesis exists
  because oscillator banks lost at ~100 partials; B1 cost $\propto f_s^2$, so it does not
  fix 96 kHz. "Skip bins below the floor" yields nothing at a −80 dB floor.
  B1 null test only holds with per-region δ. B2's −51 dBc is for a symmetric
  Hann; here 1/A tail amplification (×122) raises it. Skipped middle path:
  band-limited region synthesis (demodulate the region to baseband, IFFT of
  ~$4b$, interpolate the last $3/16$ to 384 samples), cost $\sum 4b \cdot \log b$, keeps
  per-sample control and per-region exactness; or output-pruned IFFT (~30 %).
  Note the per-sample modulator contract is self-imposed (Eventide, Whammy,
  Elastique update at block rate).
- C: MQ/PARSHL birth–death matching; known failure: causal greedy matching
  swaps tracks on crossing/beating partials, and the cure (minimum track
  length) needs lookahead. Beating pairs split/merge every half beat → $\theta=0$
  births next to $\theta \neq 0$ neighbours → caveat-5 dips at beat rate on every low
  dyad: skirt crackle exchanged for full-level AM. 12 dB gate re-births on
  every pick; C and E both own onset re-anchoring — define one owner.
- D: 5 Hz HPF standard, holds [but see consumer/practitioner on unity].
  $(1-\cos)/4$ is periodic $\mathrm{Hann}/2$ and sums exactly to 1 at $H=L/4$. Low-side
  taper is the wrong fix: with taper 1 a bin whose destination goes negative
  folds to $|\omega+\delta|$ through $\mathrm{Re}(z \cdot e^{j\delta n})$ (427), which is Laroche–Dolson's
  conjugate reflection; zeroing (392) produces the measured low-tone loss.
  Measure drop vs fold vs taper on E2 −12. Policy A is the sub-band-bypass
  trap (re-emits E1/B0 lower skirts unshifted); with bass under investigation
  it is a dead end.
- E: offline flux works with ±1–3 frame peak picking; causal ratio-to-median
  (Duxbury) has the known vibrato/bend false-positive rate; Rubber Band R3
  and Röbel classify per bin, not per band, because band flux fires on one
  bent partial. Band widening at 80–250 Hz = Kraft's global reset. $\theta=0$ is not
  "correct" for a translated partial; the lag is window weighting $A \to 0.008$,
  which neither θ nor widening changes.
- F: no shipped real-time shifter resolves sub-2-bin dyads; all accept the
  warble — evidence the problem is hard, not unimportant: at −7 st an
  unresolved E2+G#2 region puts both notes ~100 cents off and widens the
  third by ~170 cents (arithmetic from line 387). Envelope reuse re-imposes
  the merged beat (two beat rates at once); $\psi_i$ undefined (zoom phase refers
  to a centroid ~170 ms old; 0.5 zoom-bin error → 1.6 rad); 341 ms is sized
  for E2/F2 — E2/G#2 (21 Hz) needs only ~85 ms ($N=4096$, 11.7 Hz bins) and
  settles 4× faster: use two scales, declare minor seconds out of scope like
  B0/C1.
- G: Keiler–Marchand 2002: phase-difference estimators degrade fastest with a
  close interferer. Skipped alternative: spectral reassignment (one extra FFT
  of the derivative-weighted frame per hop, ω per bin, lobe spread = free
  "unresolved" flag). Check first whether the E2 bias is the negative-
  frequency image biasing both estimators [it is — confirmed by the probe].
- H: the historical failure mode of phase-vocoder products is the heuristic
  stack tuned one fixture at a time (Rubber Band R2→R3 was a rewrite for that
  reason). Measure before A, profile before B, move F's estimator ahead of E.
  Parity with the static Engine is lost only when per-bin δ arrives.

## Synthesis

Convergence (two or more reviewers independently):

1. **A (soft $\cos^2$ masks) fails as proposed** — all four: a ramp bin shared by
   regions with $\theta_p \neq \theta_q$ has amplitude $|w+(1-w)e^{j\Delta\theta}|$ (steady notch/beat), the
   skirt is emitted at two frequencies, the ramp is degenerate at the 2–3.5 bin
   spacings of every low harmonic series, and births/deaths still step. The
   fixture run also refuted the churn crackle it targeted. **Dropped.** The
   only structural fix for owner-change steps is per-bin phase state; revisit
   only if a fixture shows the step audible.
2. **C (gated ancestry with $\theta=0$ births, zombies, relative hysteresis) fails** —
   all four: births at beat nulls and threshold crossings produce the caveat-5
   dip at beat rate on the primary material; hysteresis against a moving
   `largest` is not hysteresis; C before G re-births on IF toggles. **Dropped
   as proposed.** Keep the formalist's total rule: ancestor := the previous
   region that owned bin $k_p$ (no gate, no $\theta=0$ birth, no new constants).
3. **D-policy-B (HPF inside the engine) breaks the exact-unity contract** —
   three reviewers. **No HPF.** DC offsets stay out of scope for guitar. Keep
   the POU DC term with the code's $(1-\cos)/4$ and $/\mathrm{mean}(A)$ gain; replace
   the sub-zero *drop* with the fold (taper 1 = conjugate reflection) and
   measure gain recovery; handle the bin-0 leakage ghost (−18 dB at E1, now
   measured) via the lowest region, not a fixed sub-25 Hz region.
4. **E (band-flux onsets with widening) is a band-scoped global reset** — all
   four; the lag is window-caused and θ cannot restore it. **Deferred**; first
   measure the onset lag on the region path at $u \approx 0$ vs exact unity.
5. **F as proposed (envelope-reusing parametric low band) re-imposes the old
   beat and has an undefined phase splice** — all four. But the historian and
   consumer agree the unresolved-dyad error (~100 cents on both notes of a low
   third, measured spacing unchanged in the fixture run) is the dominant
   musical defect and must move up, with a shorter second scale (≈85 ms) for
   intervals ≥ 20 Hz. **Re-scoped**: evaluate a multi-resolution low band
   (longer past-heavy analysis, same support, same synthesis window, same
   transport) before any parametric synthesis.
6. **G holds** — all four accept it for isolated tones; the historian's
   image-interference diagnosis is confirmed numerically. **Adopt** the
   image-aware lobe fit; state in the acceptance that it is a single-sinusoid
   fit and does nothing for unresolved dyads.
7. **B1 is not a CPU win for guitar** (practitioner, historian): break-even
   ≈28 regions, cost $\propto f_s^2$, and the hop-boundary analysis burst is untouched.
   **Dropped.** B2 violates the contract (all four). Cheap profiling targets
   first: the 2048-complex clear per region per channel (line 418) and the
   audio-thread sort (274); band-limited or output-pruned region IFFTs later.
8. **Missing items that all four ranked above A–H**: the unity-transition dip
   (now confirmed, −15 dB per partial) and the alias/low-side handling from
   the support maximum. Step 0 (record current numbers for every fixture;
   gate on relative change) is done by `scripts/quality_fixtures.py`.

Disagreements to surface: practitioner wants an internal tuning struct and a
per-region trace ring before any change (diagnosability); ponytail says add
them only when a regression needs them. Historian questions the per-sample
control contract itself (block-rate pitch is industry norm) — a ShiftKey
decision, not taken here.

Resulting order: unity-transition fix → G (image-aware estimator) + ownership
ancestry → D (POU DC gain, fold, bin-0 ghost) → window-tail study (skirt
sidebands at −19 dB and spacing-independent hop lines at −41…−47 dB) →
multi-resolution low band → profiling → E and stereo refinements last.
